# PH Medications: Major Treatment Pathways

Pulmonary hypertension treatment is individualized. For pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH), specialists may use more than one treatment pathway when combination therapy is appropriate.

## Prostacyclin pathway

These therapies support prostacyclin signaling involved in blood-vessel relaxation, blood flow, and vascular cell growth. Delivery may be intravenous, subcutaneous, inhaled, oral, or injected depending on the drug.

Examples listed by Breathtaking Awareness include **epoprostenol (Flolan, Veletri), iloprost (Ventavis), treprostinil therapies such as Remodulin, Tyvaso, Orenitram and Yutrepia, and selexipag (Uptravi).**

## Endothelin receptor antagonists (ERAs)

ERAs block the effects of endothelin, a substance that can narrow blood vessels. Examples include **ambrisentan (Letairis/Volibris), macitentan (Opsumit), and bosentan (Tracleer).**

## Nitric-oxide pathway

PDE-5 inhibitors help support blood-vessel relaxation. Examples include **sildenafil (Revatio) and tadalafil (Adcirca).** Soluble guanylate cyclase (sGC) stimulators are another treatment pathway used in selected forms of PH.

## Activin signaling inhibitor

**Sotatercept-csrk (Winrevair)** is an injectable activin signaling inhibitor used in adults with PAH.
