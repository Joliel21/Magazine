# The Five Clinical Groups of PH

Pulmonary hypertension is organized into five clinical groups based mainly on the underlying cause or associated condition.

## Group 1 — Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension (PAH)

Includes idiopathic and heritable PAH; drug- or toxin-associated PAH; and PAH associated with connective tissue disease, HIV, portal hypertension, congenital heart disease, schistosomiasis, or venous/capillary involvement.

## Group 2 — PH Associated With Left Heart Disease

Includes PH related to heart failure with preserved, mildly reduced, or reduced ejection fraction; valvular heart disease; and other cardiovascular conditions that lead to post-capillary PH.

## Group 3 — PH Associated With Lung Disease and/or Hypoxia

Includes obstructive or restrictive lung disease, mixed lung disease, hypoventilation syndromes, hypoxia without lung disease, and developmental lung disorders.

## Group 4 — PH Associated With Pulmonary Artery Obstructions

Includes chronic thromboembolic pulmonary hypertension (CTEPH) and other pulmonary artery obstructions.

## Group 5 — PH With Unclear and/or Multifactorial Mechanisms

Includes selected systemic and metabolic disorders, chronic kidney failure, pulmonary tumor thrombotic microangiopathy, fibrosing mediastinitis, and other complex causes.
