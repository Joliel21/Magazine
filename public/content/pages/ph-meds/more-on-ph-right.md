# Functional Class: How PH Affects Daily Life

The World Health Organization (WHO) functional class describes how much pulmonary hypertension symptoms limit activity. It is different from the five PH clinical groups.

## Class I

No symptoms with ordinary physical activity.

## Class II

Comfortable at rest, but ordinary activity may cause shortness of breath, fatigue, chest pain, or lightheadedness.

## Class III

Comfortable at rest, but less-than-ordinary activity may cause symptoms. Normal daily activities become more limited.

## Class IV

Symptoms may be present at rest, and any physical activity can increase discomfort. This is the most severe functional class.

## Know both your group and your class

Your **PH group** describes the likely cause or type of pulmonary hypertension. Your **functional class** describes how symptoms affect what you can do. Both can influence conversations about risk, treatment, and follow-up.

Treatment is individualized. Depending on the PH group and the person, care may include PH-targeted medication, treatment of an underlying condition, oxygen, rehabilitation or supervised activity, procedures, lifestyle adjustments, and ongoing specialist monitoring.
